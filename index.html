What is NERSI?

NERSI stands for National Emergency Relief and Stabilisation Intervention. It is a Federal Government programme managed by the Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction.

Selected poor and vulnerable households receive ₦50,000 support paid into a mobile wallet. The money is a grant (not a loan).

Key Official Facts

  • You cannot apply or register online for NERSI.
  • You cannot pay anyone to be added to the list.
  • Beneficiaries are selected from existing government records (National Social Register and verified national identity data).
  • Selected people receive an SMS from an approved payment provider.
  • The programme is completely free. No fees are charged by the government.
  • Payments are made in phases — not everyone is contacted at the same time.

How the Real Process Works

  1. If you are selected, you will receive an SMS from one of these approved providers: ALAT (Wema), MoMo PSB, NairaGram, OPay, or Moniepoint.
  2. The message will ask you to give consent (usually by replying YES or dialling a USSD code).
  3. After you accept, ₦50,000 is credited to a mobile wallet linked to your phone number.
  4. You can then withdraw the money through a POS agent, transfer it to a bank account, or spend from the wallet.

Genuine messages never ask for your PIN, OTP, BVN, or money, and they do not contain random website links.

How to Spot Scam Websites

These are common signs of fake NERSI sites:

  • Pages that ask you to “Enter your name”, “Activate account”, or fill a form
  • Fake counters showing “X number of people have received ₦50,000 today”
  • Progress bars or “Please wait…” screens that lead to more pages
  • Requests for bank details, NIN, BVN, or payment to “process” the money
  • WhatsApp or Facebook links telling you to share the message with friends before you can claim
  • Sites using official-looking logos or claiming to be the “NERSI portal”
  • Any site that is not https://fmhapr.gov.ng/nersi

If you see any of the above → it is a scam. Do not enter any information.

What You Should Do

  • Only trust information from the official Ministry page: fmhapr.gov.ng/nersi
  • If you receive a suspicious SMS or call, do not reply or share any details.
  • Call the official helpline 0700-022-2222 if you are unsure.
  • Never share your PIN, OTP, or BVN with anyone claiming to help you receive the money.
  • Do not click unknown links shared on WhatsApp or social media.

Checking Eligibility (Optional)

Some payment providers allow you to check status via USSD. These codes have been publicly shared:

  • ALAT by Wema: *945*23#
  • MoMo PSB: *671*0#
  • NairaGram: *726*00#
  • OPay: *955*20#
  • Moniepoint: *5573*27#

Even if the code says you are not selected, that does not mean you can never be selected later — payments go out in phases.
