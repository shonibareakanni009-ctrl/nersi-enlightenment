Privacy Policy

NERSI ₦50,000 Cash Support — Independent Public Information Guide

In short: This site does not provide forms, accounts, or a way to apply for NERSI. We do not ask you to submit personal information.

About this policy

This policy explains privacy practices for this independent educational website (the “site”), which shares publicly available information intended to help people recognize and avoid NERSI-related scams. The site is not affiliated with the Government of Nigeria or the official NERSI programme.

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Information we collect

The site has no registration, application, contact, or payment forms. We do not intentionally collect or request names, phone numbers, bank details, NIN, BVN, passwords, or other information that identifies you.

When you visit a website, its hosting provider may automatically process basic technical information (such as an IP address, browser, and request time) in server logs for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. We do not use this site to build a profile of visitors. For details about server logs and retention, consult the hosting provider’s privacy information.

Cookies, analytics, and local features

This site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include an analytics service. The share links open third-party services only when you choose to use them. The “Copy Link” feature uses your browser’s clipboard after you click it; your browser may ask for permission. The site does not receive the copied link contents.

Third-party websites

Links to the official Ministry page and social-sharing services take you to websites operated by others. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one. Check the destination address and the provider’s policy before sharing information. We do not control or endorse third-party privacy practices.

Children and security

This is general public information, not an account-based service and not directed at collecting information from children. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure; do not send sensitive information through links or contact routes that claim to represent this site.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date above indicates the latest revision. No contact email or data-submission channel is currently provided by this site.
